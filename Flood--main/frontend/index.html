Hydraulic Physics Engines

1. Manning's Equation

v = (1/n) · R2/3 · S1/2

Calculates channel cross-sectional water velocity given bed roughness coefficient n, hydraulic radius R, and valley slope gradient S.

2. Dam Rule-Curve Surcharge

Upstream reservoirs (e.g. Tehri, Pandoh, Bhakra) release surplus spillway discharge whenever reservoir storage exceeds monsoon rule curves to avoid crest overtopping.

3. Rain-on-Ice GLOF Trigger

Cloudburst intensity (>25 mm/h) over high-altitude moraine lakes accelerates glacier melt via sensible heat transfer, releasing sudden debris surges.

4. SCS Runoff Volume

Soil moisture exceeding 80% reduces infiltration to near zero, converting 85-94% of incident rainfall directly into surface sheetwash.
